Understand your LLPIN.

Validate the format of LLP Identification Numbers: three letters, a hyphen and four digits, like AAA-1234. An LLPIN does not encode state, year or type.

This tool validates identifier structure and decodes encoded fields. It does not verify company or LLP existence, registration status, or current listing status against the MCA registry.
